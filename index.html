Restoring purchases

Story packs are bought once and are yours to keep. On a new device, open any paid pack, tap Unlock, answer the grown-ups question, then tap Restore Purchases.

Family Sharing

Every pack, and the all-packs bundle, can be shared with your Family Sharing group. Family members get them automatically, or by tapping Restore Purchases.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Paper Pockets collects no personal information: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
